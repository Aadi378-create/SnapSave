# New research engine: independent trial runs

25 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata. All order creation, research imports, messages and quotes in these trials used disposable databases and test identities. Retailer captures were controlled fixtures, not new live retailer observations. No real customer order, payment, retailer account or cart was changed.

## Completed scenario

The browser trial exercised the real application DOM and background executor end to end:

1. Open the customer screen, click **New Order**, type `2x Amul milk 1 L, Epigamia High Protein Paneer 200 g`, and press Enter.
2. The saved order starts research, the background worker completes its plan, and the customer sees 0/2 matches. No price or quote is generated by planning.
3. Import an order/revision/postcode-bound Zepto fixture: coverage becomes 1/2.
4. Import the Instamart fixture: coverage becomes 2/2 and the first retailer's evidence remains present. The order remains `pending_review`, final price remains zero, and no quote is published merely because matches exist.
5. Open the management screen and confirm both matching products and fresh coverage are visible, without a stale failure warning.
6. Submit an explicit human quote: two milk packs at the fixture's ₹77 each produce a ₹154 line, plus ₹150 paneer. Subtotal is ₹304. The configured test fee calculation produces ₹70 delivery and ₹5 platform fee, so the test final price is ₹379. These are fixture/test values, not a current retailer offer or real payment request.
7. Reload the customer screen: the verified human quote appears, the order is `awaiting_customer`, and the earlier research-progress panel no longer competes with quote review.

Screenshots from this disposable run: `customer-plan.png`, `admin-research.png`, `customer-quote.png`. External fonts/icons are mocked in the isolated browser, so screenshot typography is not a production visual certification.

## Issues reproduced and fixed

| Finding | Reproduction | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero quantity silently became one | `0x milk`, `0 pieces bread`, `0 packets rice` created a one-item order | The cart parser defaults only missing quantities to one. Explicit zero reaches existing API validation and returns HTTP 422 without an order or research job. |
| Simultaneous identical retries returned a conflict | Two requests with the same customer, key and list raced during the initial database flush | The uniqueness recovery now covers the initial flush as well as commit. Both requests return the same saved order; exactly one customer list and one research generation are recorded. |
| Stale research failure text survived success | Management showed two fresh matches beside “No usable public catalogue prices were available” | Browser planning is treated as waiting for capture rather than a failure. Successful capture import clears an older AI error, leaving coverage and human-review instructions authoritative. |

The first five-trial round passed the end-to-end scenario and reproduced four failures (three zero-quantity variants plus the concurrent retry). `initial-results.xml` keeps the outcomes with raw fixture-response/stack details omitted. The five trials passed after the first two fixes (`fixed-results.xml`). Screenshot inspection identified the third issue, and the final trial now asserts that planned and successfully imported research have no stale error.

## Scope and remaining limits

The trials establish that the engine wiring, actual background execution, typed-input flow, research accumulation, human quote gate and both screens work together under the tested conditions. The broader suite also checks 100-item lists, unsupported names, expired evidence, role/security boundaries, idempotency, global AI shutdown, draft handling and payment state guards.

This test run does not establish unattended access to retailers, exact-address delivery or live stock/fees. The installed toolbar still needs a staff click and fresh relevant-page captures, and ticket import remains manual. Items absent from captures remain unresolved rather than receiving a guessed price. Zepto's Kudasan location-search issue from the previous live investigation remains unresolved. Historical captures were not retimestamped or presented as fresh retailer data.

One existing Starlette/httpx deprecation warning remains.
